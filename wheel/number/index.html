    Random Number Wheel

    Set a range, spin, and get a random number. Perfect for raffles, picking a student, board games or any time you need a number with a bit of drama.

    1. Type or paste your entries, one per line.
    2. Click Spin (or tap the wheel) and wait for it to stop.
    3. The winner pops up. Remove it and spin again, or share the wheel with a link.
    What range can I use?
    Any whole numbers, from 1 up to 1,000 slices on the wheel.
    Can the same number come up twice?
    Yes by default. Turn on “Remove winner after spin” to take each number out once it's picked.
