About Ivory

The warm off-white of elephant tusk ivory; a classic choice for wedding stationery and soft backgrounds.

  • Palette: pair it with Alice Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Floral White and Honeydew for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (20.81:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as ivory or #fffff0.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 0%, 6%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
