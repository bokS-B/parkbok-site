About Medium Sea Green

A balanced medium green with a hint of blue, often used for success states.

  • Palette: pair it with Medium Violet Red as a contrasting accent, or with Lime Green and Light Sea Green for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (7.88:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as mediumseagreen or #3cb371.
  • Print: about cmyk(66%, 0%, 37%, 30%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
