About Turquoise

Named after the gemstone, from the French for 'Turkish', as the stone reached Europe through Turkey.

  • Palette: pair it with Indian Red as a contrasting accent, or with Emerald and Twitter Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (12.79:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as turquoise or #40e0d0.
  • Print: about cmyk(71%, 0%, 7%, 12%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
