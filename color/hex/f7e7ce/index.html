About Champagne

A pale golden beige named after the sparkling wine; common in wedding and luxury design.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Pastel Pink and Light Goldenrod Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (17.28:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #f7e7ce.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 6%, 17%, 3%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
