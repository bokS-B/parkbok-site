About Rosy Brown

A greyish dusty rose, somewhere between pink and brown.

  • Palette: pair it with Pastel Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Pastel Purple and Sage Green for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (7.46:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as rosybrown or #bc8f8f.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 24%, 24%, 26%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
