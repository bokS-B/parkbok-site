About Netflix Red

Widely cited as the brand red of Netflix.

  • Palette: pair it with Dark Turquoise as a contrasting accent, or with Ruby and Chocolate for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (4.79:1, AA). Write it in CSS as #e50914.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 96%, 91%, 10%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
  • Brand names only identify the color; parkbok is not affiliated with the brand owner, and official brand guidelines take precedence.
