About Dark Blue

Blue at a little over half intensity: a deep, ink-like blue that keeps strong contrast with white text.

  • Palette: pair it with Olive as a contrasting accent, or with Cobalt Blue and Indigo for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (15.30:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as darkblue or #00008b.
  • Print: about cmyk(100%, 100%, 0%, 45%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
