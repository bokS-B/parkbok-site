About Linen

The off-white of undyed linen cloth woven from flax.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Misty Rose and Old Lace for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (18.67:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as linen or #faf0e6.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 4%, 8%, 2%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
