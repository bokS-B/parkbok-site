About Sandy Brown

A light orange-brown evoking beach sand.

  • Palette: pair it with Cornflower Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Tomato and Mustard Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (10.33:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as sandybrown or #f4a460.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 33%, 61%, 4%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
