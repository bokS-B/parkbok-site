About Papaya Whip

Named after a dessert of whipped papaya; a pale peach cream.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Pastel Pink and Light Goldenrod Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (18.56:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as papayawhip or #ffefd5.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 6%, 16%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
