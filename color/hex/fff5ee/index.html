About Seashell

A warm white with a pinkish tint, like the inside of a seashell.

  • Palette: pair it with Alice Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Lavender Blush and Ivory for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (19.55:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as seashell or #fff5ee.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 4%, 7%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
