About Cornsilk

The pale yellow of the silky threads at the tip of an ear of corn.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Misty Rose and Light Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (19.71:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as cornsilk or #fff8dc.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 3%, 14%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
