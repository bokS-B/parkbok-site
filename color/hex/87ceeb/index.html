About Sky Blue

The soft blue of a clear daytime sky; a staple for light, friendly designs.

  • Palette: pair it with Dark Salmon as a contrasting accent, or with Aquamarine and Cornflower Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (12.06:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as skyblue or #87ceeb.
  • Print: about cmyk(43%, 12%, 0%, 8%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
