About Crimson

From the kermes insect dye (Arabic qirmiz). A deep red long used for ceremonial robes, flags and university colors.

  • Palette: pair it with Dark Turquoise as a contrasting accent, or with Medium Violet Red and Chocolate for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (4.99:1, AA). Write it in CSS as crimson or #dc143c.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 91%, 73%, 14%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
