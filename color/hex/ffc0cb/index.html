About Pink

The color is named after pinks (Dianthus) — flowers whose frilled petals look as if cut with pinking shears.

  • Palette: pair it with Pale Turquoise as a contrasting accent, or with Pastel Pink and Peach Puff for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (13.65:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as pink or #ffc0cb.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 25%, 20%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
