About Terracotta

From the Italian for 'baked earth', the orange-brown of unglazed fired clay.

  • Palette: pair it with Medium Turquoise as a contrasting accent, or with Pale Violet Red and Sandy Brown for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (6.79:1, AA). Write it in CSS as #e2725b.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 50%, 60%, 11%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
