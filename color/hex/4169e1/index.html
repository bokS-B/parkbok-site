About Royal Blue

A rich, vivid blue traditionally associated with royal households and formal uniforms.

  • Palette: pair it with Goldenrod as a contrasting accent, or with Medium Turquoise and Slate Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (4.85:1, AA). Write it in CSS as royalblue or #4169e1.
  • Print: about cmyk(71%, 53%, 0%, 12%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
