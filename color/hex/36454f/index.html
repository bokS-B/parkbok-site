About Charcoal

A dark blue-grey named after burnt wood charcoal; a softer alternative to black for text and dark themes.

  • Palette: pair it with Dark Slate Gray as a contrasting accent. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (9.90:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #36454f.
  • Print: about cmyk(32%, 13%, 0%, 69%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
