About Pale Green

A light, fresh pastel green.

  • Palette: pair it with Violet as a contrasting accent, or with Pale Goldenrod and Aquamarine for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (16.59:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as palegreen or #98fb98.
  • Print: about cmyk(39%, 0%, 39%, 2%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
