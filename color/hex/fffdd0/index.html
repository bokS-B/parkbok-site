About Cream

The pale yellowish white of dairy cream; softer than pure white for paper-like designs.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Blanched Almond and Light Goldenrod Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (20.21:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #fffdd0.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 1%, 18%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
