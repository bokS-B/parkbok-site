About Pastel Pink

A soft, light pink and one of the most searched pastel tones, used for baby products, cosmetics and gentle UI accents.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Cyan as a contrasting accent, or with Misty Rose and Bisque for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (15.41:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #ffd1dc.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 18%, 14%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
