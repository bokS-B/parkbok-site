About Dark Slate Blue

A dark blue-violet named after the bluish tone of slate rock.

  • Palette: pair it with Olive Drab as a contrasting accent, or with Dim Gray and Rebecca Purple for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (9.07:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as darkslateblue or #483d8b.
  • Print: about cmyk(48%, 56%, 0%, 45%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
