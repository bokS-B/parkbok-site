About Sienna

Named after the earth pigment from the area around Siena, Italy, used by painters since the Renaissance.

  • Palette: pair it with Steel Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Brown and Dark Goldenrod for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (5.62:1, AA). Write it in CSS as sienna or #a0522d.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 49%, 72%, 37%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
