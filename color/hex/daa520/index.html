About Goldenrod

Named after goldenrod (Solidago), a North American wildflower with dense yellow flower heads.

  • Palette: pair it with LinkedIn Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Chocolate and Yellow Green for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (9.38:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as goldenrod or #daa520.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 24%, 85%, 15%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
