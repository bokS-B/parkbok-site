About Sea Green

A medium-dark green with a blue tint, suggesting deep sea water.

  • Palette: pair it with Brown as a contrasting accent, or with Forest Green and Dark Cyan for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (4.95:1, AA). Write it in CSS as seagreen or #2e8b57.
  • Print: about cmyk(67%, 0%, 37%, 45%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
