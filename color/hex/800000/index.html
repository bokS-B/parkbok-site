About Maroon

From the French marron, 'chestnut'. A deep brownish red and one of the 16 original HTML color names.

  • Palette: pair it with Teal as a contrasting accent, or with Burgundy and Saddle Brown for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (10.95:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as maroon or #800000.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 100%, 100%, 50%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
