About WhatsApp Green

Widely cited as the brand green of WhatsApp.

  • Palette: pair it with Medium Violet Red as a contrasting accent, or with Lime Green and Turquoise for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (10.59:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #25d366.
  • Print: about cmyk(82%, 0%, 52%, 17%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
  • Brand names only identify the color; parkbok is not affiliated with the brand owner, and official brand guidelines take precedence.
