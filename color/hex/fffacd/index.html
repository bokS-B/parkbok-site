About Lemon Chiffon

Named after lemon chiffon pie, with its light, pale-yellow filling.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Bisque and Light Goldenrod Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (19.81:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as lemonchiffon or #fffacd.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 2%, 20%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
