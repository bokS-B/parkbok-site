About Baby Blue

A light blue traditionally used for baby clothing and nurseries.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Salmon as a contrasting accent, or with Aquamarine and Cornflower Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (12.25:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #89cff0.
  • Print: about cmyk(43%, 14%, 0%, 6%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
