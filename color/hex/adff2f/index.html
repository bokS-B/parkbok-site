About Green Yellow

A bright yellow-green, between chartreuse and yellow, that reads as fresh and electric on screen.

  • Palette: pair it with Blue Violet as a contrasting accent, or with Mustard Yellow and Neon Green for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (17.12:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as greenyellow or #adff2f.
  • Print: about cmyk(32%, 0%, 82%, 0%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
