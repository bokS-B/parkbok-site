About Light Steel Blue

A pale grey-blue suggesting polished steel.

  • Palette: pair it with Wheat as a contrasting accent, or with Light Blue and Pastel Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (11.80:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as lightsteelblue or #b0c4de.
  • Print: about cmyk(21%, 12%, 0%, 13%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
