About Light Cyan

A very pale cyan, close to white with a cool aqua tint.

  • Palette: pair it with Misty Rose as a contrasting accent, or with Honeydew and Lavender for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (19.92:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as lightcyan or #e0ffff.
  • Print: about cmyk(12%, 0%, 0%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
