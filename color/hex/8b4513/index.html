About Saddle Brown

The warm brown of tanned leather saddles.

  • Palette: pair it with Cobalt Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Burgundy and Olive for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (7.10:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as saddlebrown or #8b4513.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 50%, 86%, 45%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
