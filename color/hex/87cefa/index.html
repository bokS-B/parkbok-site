About Light Sky Blue

A soft daytime-sky blue, slightly more saturated than 'skyblue'.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Salmon as a contrasting accent, or with Aquamarine and Cornflower Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (12.24:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as lightskyblue or #87cefa.
  • Print: about cmyk(46%, 18%, 0%, 2%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
