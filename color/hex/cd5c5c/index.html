About Indian Red

Named after a red iron-oxide earth pigment historically imported from India.

  • Palette: pair it with Medium Turquoise as a contrasting accent, or with Pale Violet Red and Peru for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (5.28:1, AA). Write it in CSS as indianred or #cd5c5c.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 55%, 55%, 20%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
