About Pale Violet Red

A dusty rose pink between violet and red.

  • Palette: pair it with Medium Aquamarine as a contrasting accent, or with Orchid and Light Coral for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (6.75:1, AA). Write it in CSS as palevioletred or #db7093.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 49%, 33%, 14%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
