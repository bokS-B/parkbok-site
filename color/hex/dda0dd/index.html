About Plum

A light purple named after the fruit, though much paler than a ripe plum's skin.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Green as a contrasting accent, or with Lilac for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (10.15:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as plum or #dda0dd.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 28%, 0%, 13%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
