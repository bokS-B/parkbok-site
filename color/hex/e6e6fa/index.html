About Lavender

Named after the pale purple flowers of the lavender plant; a soft, calming tint.

  • Palette: pair it with Old Lace as a contrasting accent, or with Alice Blue and White Smoke for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (17.06:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as lavender or #e6e6fa.
  • Print: about cmyk(8%, 8%, 0%, 2%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
