About Salmon

Named after the pink-orange color of salmon flesh.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Sky Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Hot Pink and Mustard Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (8.40:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as salmon or #fa8072.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 49%, 54%, 2%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
