About Honeydew

Named after the pale green flesh of the honeydew melon.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender Blush as a contrasting accent, or with Ivory and Mint Cream for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (20.27:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as honeydew or #f0fff0.
  • Print: about cmyk(6%, 0%, 6%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
