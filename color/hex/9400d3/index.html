About Dark Violet

A vivid violet with no green component, close to the violet end of the visible spectrum.

  • Palette: pair it with Lime Green as a contrasting accent, or with Medium Blue and Medium Violet Red for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (6.56:1, AA). Write it in CSS as darkviolet or #9400d3.
  • Print: about cmyk(30%, 100%, 0%, 17%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
