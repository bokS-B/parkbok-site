About Mustard Yellow

A warm, slightly dull yellow named after prepared mustard; popular in fashion and retro designs.

  • Palette: pair it with Google Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Coral and Green Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (15.53:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #ffdb58.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 14%, 65%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
