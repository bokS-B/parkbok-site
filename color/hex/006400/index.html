About Dark Green

A deep natural green with the green channel at about 39%, darker than the CSS keyword 'green'.

  • Palette: pair it with Indigo as a contrasting accent, or with Green and Starbucks Green for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (7.44:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as darkgreen or #006400.
  • Print: about cmyk(100%, 0%, 100%, 61%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
