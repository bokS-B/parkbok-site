About Dark Goldenrod

A darker, browner relative of goldenrod, named after the yellow-flowered Solidago plant.

  • Palette: pair it with Cobalt Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Firebrick and Yellow Green for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (6.45:1, AA). Write it in CSS as darkgoldenrod or #b8860b.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 27%, 94%, 28%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
