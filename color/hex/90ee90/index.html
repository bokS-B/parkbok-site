About Light Green

A soft pastel green, slightly deeper than 'palegreen'.

  • Palette: pair it with Violet as a contrasting accent, or with Pale Green and Aquamarine for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (14.82:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as lightgreen or #90ee90.
  • Print: about cmyk(39%, 0%, 39%, 7%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
