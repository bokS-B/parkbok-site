About Old Lace

The warm off-white of aged lace; a gentle alternative to pure white.

  • Palette: pair it with Lavender as a contrasting accent, or with Misty Rose and Light Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (19.38:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as oldlace or #fdf5e6.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 3%, 9%, 1%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
