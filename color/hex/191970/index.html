About Midnight Blue

A very dark navy suggesting the night sky; a favorite for dark themes and formal designs.

  • Palette: pair it with Dark Olive Green as a contrasting accent, or with Dark Slate Gray and Indigo for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (14.85:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as midnightblue or #191970.
  • Print: about cmyk(78%, 78%, 0%, 56%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
