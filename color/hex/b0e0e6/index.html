About Powder Blue

A soft, chalky pale blue, popular for nurseries, calm interfaces and spring palettes.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Pink as a contrasting accent, or with Light Blue and Light Steel Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (14.65:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as powderblue or #b0e0e6.
  • Print: about cmyk(23%, 3%, 0%, 10%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
