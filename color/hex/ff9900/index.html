About Amazon Orange

Widely cited as the orange of the Amazon smile logo.

  • Palette: pair it with Facebook Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Red and Yellow for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (9.81:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #ff9900.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 40%, 100%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
  • Brand names only identify the color; parkbok is not affiliated with the brand owner, and official brand guidelines take precedence.
