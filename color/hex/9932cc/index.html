About Dark Orchid

A darker, more saturated variant of orchid, named after the orchid flower.

  • Palette: pair it with Lime Green as a contrasting accent, or with Rebecca Purple and Medium Violet Red for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (5.70:1, AA). Write it in CSS as darkorchid or #9932cc.
  • Print: about cmyk(25%, 75%, 0%, 20%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
