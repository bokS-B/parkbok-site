About Yellow

Full red plus full green light. Yellow is also one of the four CMYK process inks.

  • Palette: pair it with Blue as a contrasting accent, or with Dark Orange and Chartreuse for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (19.56:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as yellow or #ffff00.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 0%, 100%, 0%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
