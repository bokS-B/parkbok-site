About Steel Blue

A medium blue-grey suggesting tempered steel; a versatile corporate blue.

  • Palette: pair it with Peru as a contrasting accent, or with Light Sea Green and Dark Slate Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (5.11:1, AA). Write it in CSS as steelblue or #4682b4.
  • Print: about cmyk(61%, 28%, 0%, 29%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
