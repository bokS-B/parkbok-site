About Pale Turquoise

A light, airy blue-green named after turquoise.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Pink as a contrasting accent, or with Powder Blue and Light Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (16.29:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as paleturquoise or #afeeee.
  • Print: about cmyk(26%, 0%, 0%, 7%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
