About Hot Pink

A bold, playful pink that became a pop-culture staple; brighter and warmer than 'deeppink'.

  • Palette: pair it with Aquamarine as a contrasting accent, or with Violet and Salmon for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (7.93:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as hotpink or #ff69b4.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 59%, 29%, 0%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
