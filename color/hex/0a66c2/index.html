About LinkedIn Blue

Widely cited as the brand blue of LinkedIn.

  • Palette: pair it with Chocolate as a contrasting accent, or with Tiffany Blue and Medium Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (5.69:1, AA). Write it in CSS as #0a66c2.
  • Print: about cmyk(95%, 47%, 0%, 24%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
  • Brand names only identify the color; parkbok is not affiliated with the brand owner, and official brand guidelines take precedence.
