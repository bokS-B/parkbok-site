About Firebrick

Named after the red of fired clay bricks; a strong, slightly brown red.

  • Palette: pair it with Light Sea Green as a contrasting accent, or with Medium Violet Red and Chocolate for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: white text reads best on it (6.68:1, AA). Write it in CSS as firebrick or #b22222.
  • Print: about cmyk(0%, 81%, 81%, 30%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
