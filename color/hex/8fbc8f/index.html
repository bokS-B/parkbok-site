About Dark Sea Green

A soft, greyish green meant to suggest shallow sea water; useful as a calm accent.

  • Palette: pair it with Pastel Purple as a contrasting accent, or with Sage Green and Dark Gray for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (9.76:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as darkseagreen or #8fbc8f.
  • Print: about cmyk(24%, 0%, 24%, 26%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
