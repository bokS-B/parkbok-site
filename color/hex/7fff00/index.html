About Chartreuse

Named after Chartreuse, the herbal liqueur made by Carthusian monks since the 18th century, whose green version has this yellow-green hue.

  • Palette: pair it with Dark Violet as a contrasting accent, or with Yellow and Lime for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (16.21:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as chartreuse or #7fff00.
  • Print: about cmyk(50%, 0%, 100%, 0%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
