About Medium Orchid

A medium purple-pink named after orchid flowers.

  • Palette: pair it with Pastel Green as a contrasting accent, or with Slate Blue and Pale Violet Red for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (5.33:1, AA). Write it in CSS as mediumorchid or #ba55d3.
  • Print: about cmyk(12%, 60%, 0%, 17%); it falls within the usual CMYK range, but check a printed proof because paper and ink shift the tone.
