About Twitter Blue

The light blue long used by Twitter before its 2023 rebrand to X.

  • Palette: pair it with Chocolate as a contrasting accent, or with Turquoise and Blue for a softer, analogous scheme. Lighter and darker steps are in the shade row above.
  • Web: black text reads best on it (7.43:1, AAA). Write it in CSS as #1da1f2.
  • Print: about cmyk(88%, 33%, 0%, 5%); a screen color this saturated sits outside the standard CMYK gamut and will print duller, so check a proof or choose a Pantone match.
  • Brand names only identify the color; parkbok is not affiliated with the brand owner, and official brand guidelines take precedence.
