How it works

Everything is calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Which formats can I paste?
HEX (#f53, #ff5733), rgb() and rgba(), hsl() and hsla(), hsv(), cmyk(), bare numbers like 255, 87, 51, and the 148 CSS color names such as tomato.
How do I share a color?
The page address ends with the color, for example #ff5733. Copy the address and the same color opens for anyone.
